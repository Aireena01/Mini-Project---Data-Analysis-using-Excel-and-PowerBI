# 🛍️ E-Commerce Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes **E-Commerce Sales Data** sourced from [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/chandrachudsahi/e-commerce-sales-data).  
The dataset was cleaned, transformed, and visualized using **Microsoft Excel** and **Power BI** to uncover insights into product performance, regional sales, and payment behavior.

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Transformation
Performed in **Excel**:
- Removed duplicates and blanks.
- Handled missing values in product, state, and payment method columns.
- Standardized text formats and corrected inconsistencies.
- Created calculated fields for total sales, quantity, and unit price.
- Replaced nulls with meaningful labels (e.g., “Unknown”).

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features (Power BI)
- **Sales by State and Product** – Clustered column chart showing quantity and total sales.
- **Payment Method Analysis** – Pie chart visualizing customer payment preferences.
- **Monthly Product Trends** – Bar chart tracking product replacements and returns.
- **Order Status Overview** – Donut chart summarizing shipped, delivered, and cancelled orders.
- **Category-Wise Sales** – Table comparing Electronics, Fashion, and Home categories across cities.
- **KPI Card** – Displays total sales goal achievement.
- **Date Filter** – Interactive slicer for custom time-period analysis.

---

## 🎯 Business Goals
- Identify top-selling products and high-performing regions.
- Understand customer payment preferences (Card, Net Banking, COD).
- Track monthly sales and return patterns.
- Support data-driven decisions for inventory and marketing optimization.

---

## 📈 Tools Used
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning, preparation, and preliminary analysis.
- **Power BI** – Dashboard creation, visualization, and KPI tracking.

---

## 🏁 Conclusion
The analysis highlights strong sales performance in **Delhi and Maharashtra**, with **Card and Net Banking** as preferred payment methods.  
Insights from this dashboard help improve **inventory planning, payment experience, and marketing strategies** for sustained business growth.
